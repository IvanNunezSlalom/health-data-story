# Health Equity Data Story – Project Brief

## Overview

This project is a **desktop-focused interactive data story prototype** designed to demonstrate how public health departments can communicate health equity and social determinants of health (SDOH) to non-technical stakeholders. The prototype will use **fictitious data** to illustrate how life expectancy, health outcomes, and neighborhood conditions vary across communities, and how these disparities relate to social and economic factors. Structured as a scrollable narrative with embedded visualizations, the prototype aims to make complex equity issues accessible, engaging, and actionable for colleagues, community partners, and policymakers.

The primary audience is **Avery Martinez, Health Equity Program Lead**, who coordinates cross-sector initiatives to reduce health disparities. Avery needs a tool to clearly show how health outcomes differ by neighborhood, race/ethnicity, and income; how those differences connect to SDOH; and where to prioritize interventions. This prototype serves as a proof-of-concept for how digital storytelling can bring health data to life, replacing static PDFs and PowerPoint slides with an interactive, guided experience.

## Objectives

1. **Demonstrate Health Disparities**: Clearly show variation in life expectancy and other health outcomes across neighborhoods and demographic groups.

2. **Connect Health to Social Conditions**: Illustrate the relationship between health outcomes and social determinants (e.g., poverty, education, housing cost burden) through linked visualizations.

3. **Enable Exploration**: Allow users to explore their own community through a "find your neighborhood" feature with neighborhood profiles.

4. **Guide Understanding**: Use narrative text, annotations, and progressive disclosure to walk users through the story, making it accessible to non-experts.

5. **Provide a Replicable Model**: Serve as a template for how health departments can communicate equity data in future projects.

## Success Criteria

A successful prototype will:

- **Tell a Coherent Story**: Flow naturally from introducing the problem (health inequities) → showing the patterns (maps and charts) → explaining the context (SDOH/HOI) → enabling action (explore your community).

- **Make Data Tangible**: Use clear, well-labeled visualizations that non-technical audiences can understand without specialized training.

- **Demonstrate Interactivity**: Include working hover tooltips, filters, and neighborhood selection that respond smoothly and intuitively.

- **Highlight Equity Dimensions**: Clearly show disparities by race/ethnicity, income, and geography without oversimplifying or obscuring nuance.

- **Run Reliably in a Demo**: Load quickly, work consistently on desktop browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari), and require no special setup or credentials.

- **Spark Conversation**: Prompt questions like "Why is life expectancy 15 years lower in this neighborhood?" and "What interventions could close this gap?"
